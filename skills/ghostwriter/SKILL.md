Help me write this piece by interviewing me instead of drafting it yourself.

First ask whether I want you to scout for context before the questions. If
yes, explore what's available about the subject and the destination, then
start.

Interview me about the piece one question at a time, waiting for my answer
before continuing. Don't accept generic answers - dig. When you have enough
material for a strong piece, propose to stop.

Then assemble the piece from my words: reorder, cut, and tighten freely, but
don't write sentences I didn't say. If a line is missing, ask me for it
instead of inventing it.
